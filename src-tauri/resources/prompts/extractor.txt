pending_event: extract when the user states a future plan OR asks you to remind them of something.
- Today is {today}. Compute event_date from THIS date (e.g. "tomorrow" = today+1, "next Tuesday" = the Tuesday after today).
- Short-term reminder ("remind me to drink water in 30 minutes", "提醒我3分钟后喝水", "一小时后叫我"): set title to what to remind about, and offset_minutes to the number of minutes from now (e.g. 30, 3, 60). Do NOT set event_date — the system computes the exact time itself.
- Dated future event ("interview next Friday", "exam on 2026-08-05"): set title and event_date to the ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD). Do NOT set offset_minutes.

pet_promise: extract when the user ASKS THE PET to DO something for them at a future time — this becomes the pet's own promise, which she must keep ("你明早8点叫我起床" "周五晚上给我唱首歌" "明天陪我挑礼物").
